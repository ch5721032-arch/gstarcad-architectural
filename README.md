# GstarCAD Architectural Resources

Architectural drafting resources for [GstarCAD](https://www.gstarcad.net) – layer standards, drawing symbols, linetypes and conventions that keep your drawings clean, consistent and easy to share.

## What's included

- **Layer standards** – AIA-style naming for plans, sections, details
- **Symbol libraries** – doors, windows, stairs, fixtures, elevations
- **Linetypes & text styles** – ready for architectural drawings
- **Title blocks** – A3/A4 frames with project info fields
- **Sample plans** – residential and commercial floor plans to study

## Why use standards?

Consistent layers and symbols make drawings easier to read, revise and hand off between team members and consultants. These resources follow common industry conventions and work out of the box in [GstarCAD](https://www.gstarcad.net/download).
